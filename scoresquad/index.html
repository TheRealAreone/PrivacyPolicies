Privacy Policy

ScoreSquad

Last updated: September 28, 2026
Applies to the ScoreSquad app for Android and for desktop (Windows, macOS, Linux).

The short version

ScoreSquad collects no data, sends nothing anywhere, and shares nothing with anyone — including the developer. It works entirely offline. Everything you enter stays in a database on your own device or computer, and it is deleted when you delete it or uninstall the app. There are no accounts, no ads, and no trackers.

1. Who we are

ScoreSquad is an independent scorekeeping app developed and published by Sean McLaughlin ("the developer"), the sole operator of the app. There is no company behind it and no third party involved in running it. Contact details are in section 12.

2. Information the app collects

None. ScoreSquad has no sign-up, no login, and no way for you to identify yourself to the app. It does not ask for an email address, phone number, name, location, contacts, photos, or advertising ID, and it does not read anything from your device beyond what you type into it.

The app therefore has no data to sell, share, rent, or trade, and it does not respond to any requests to do so.

3. Information stored on your device

To keep score, the app saves the game information you enter in a local SQLite database that lives in the app's private storage on your device. This data never leaves the device and is never visible to the developer.

What is stored The names you give to players, the game name and type, game settings such as target score or number of rounds, the scores entered for each player and round, and the date and time of each game session and who won.
Where it is stored On Android, in the app's private internal storage, which other apps cannot read. On desktop, in a score_tracker.db file inside the app's own folder on your computer.
Who can read it Only you, on the device or computer where you entered it.
What is not stored No account identifier, email address, phone number, advertising ID, precise or approximate location, IP address, contacts, photos, files, or usage analytics.

Offline by design ScoreSquad has no server, no sign-in, and no cloud sync. It is not possible to view or recover your data through us, because we never receive it. On Android the app does not even declare the INTERNET permission, so the operating system does not permit it to open a network connection at all. You can confirm this yourself: Android's app info screen for ScoreSquad lists the permissions the app holds, and INTERNET is not among them.

4. How your information is used

Your local data is used for one purpose only: showing your scores, your game history, and the winner while the app is open on your device. It is not used for advertising, profiling, model training, analytics, or any other purpose, because it is never transmitted anywhere it could be used for those things.

5. Sharing and disclosure

We do not share your data with anyone — no advertisers, no analytics providers, no data brokers, no social networks, no cloud hosts, and no other third parties. We do not sell or share personal information, and we have never received any to share.

Because no data reaches us, there is nothing for us to disclose in response to a request, subpoena, or legal process. If a valid legal order required disclosure of your information, we could not comply, as we do not hold it.

6. Third-party services and advertising

ScoreSquad contains no third-party SDKs, advertising networks, analytics services, crash reporters, or trackers, and it displays no ads and no sponsored content. The app is built with the Flet UI framework and the SQLAlchemy database library, which run entirely on your device and send nothing externally.

7. Data retention and deletion

Your game data is kept on your device only for as long as you keep it there. You can remove it at any time, and it is never retained by the developer:

  • Delete a game: use the delete option on a game in the app's history.
  • Delete everything: clear the app's storage or uninstall the app. On Android, uninstalling ScoreSquad also removes its database. On desktop, delete the score_tracker.db file in the app folder.
  • No recovery by us: because the app has no cloud backup and we hold no copy, deleted data cannot be restored by us or anyone else.

Your device's own operating-system backup (for example, Google backup on Android) may include the app's database. That is controlled by your device settings and your operating system vendor, not by the developer.

8. Security

Because your data is never transmitted, it cannot be intercepted, hacked, or leaked through us. It is stored only in the app's private storage. As with any software on your device, keeping your operating system and the app updated, and using a screen lock, are the best protection for the data you enter.

9. Children's privacy

ScoreSquad is a general-audience scorekeeping app and is not directed at children under 13. It collects no personal information from anyone, including children, so it is not subject to the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) or similar rules. Since the app has no accounts, there is no age or identity verification to perform, and no information is knowingly collected from minors.

10. Your rights under privacy laws

Laws such as the GDPR and the CCPA give rights over personal data that a company holds about you. ScoreSquad does not collect or hold any personal data, so there is nothing for you to request a copy of, correct, export, or delete — and no obligation to opt out of sale or sharing, because none occurs. This policy exists to make that clear, not to claim an exemption from it.

11. Changes to this policy

If the app or its data practices change, this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date at the top will change. Continuing to use the app after an update means the revised policy applies. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes on Google Play.
